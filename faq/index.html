Questions about Property maintenance

Short answers about coverage, quotes, and weekday scheduling. A section for property managers follows the general questions.

General questions

Which areas do you serve

The service area is the Greater Toronto Area, including Mississauga, Oakville, Toronto, Brampton, Vaughan, and Hamilton.

How do I get a quote

Use the quote form or the plan builder with your name, phone, email, the property address, city, service type, preferred weekday timing, a short description, and photos if you have them. You can also call 416-900-7998.

What should photos show

Show the area of the property that needs work, plus access if it is tight or gated. Photos go on the quote form. The chat assistant, when it is added, will point you back to that form for uploads.

Do I need to be at the property

Not always. Say on the form whether someone will be on site, how to access the property, and any instructions for the crew.

What are the hours

Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday. Preferred timing on the form is for weekdays only.

Is this the same as Quick Fence Repair

They are related. Fence, gate, welding, and chain-link work is handled through QuickFenceRepair.ca. This site is for property maintenance. The brand name on this site is still a placeholder.

What warranty and coverage apply

5-year warranty on all work, $5 million liability insurance, WSIB coverage. Final public wording will be confirmed before launch.

For property managers

These questions are for people who look after more than one property or who request work on behalf of an owner.

Can a property manager request work for more than one property

Yes. Send one quote request per property so the address, photos, and timing stay clear, or list each property address in the details if the work is a single combined visit. The office can advise which is simpler for your roster.

Can invoices go to a management office rather than to the property

Yes. Put the billing contact and the property address in the quote form so they are not mixed up. Payment terms are confirmed in the quote, not on this page.

How do tenants or occupants report a problem

A manager can share the quote form or the office phone. Include the property address and any access instructions. This is maintenance intake, not landlord-tenant management.

Can you work if the occupant is not present

Often yes, if access is arranged. Note lockbox, superintendent, or arrival instructions in the form details.
