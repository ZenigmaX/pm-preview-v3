The Four-Pass system

Inspect. Fix. Prevent. Report.

A named sequence for how a maintenance visit is meant to run. It is a proposed operating method, not a published service-level agreement.

01

Inspect

Walk the items you listed for that property and note what needs work before tools come out.

02

Fix

Complete the agreed repairs and upkeep on a weekday visit, at the address on the request.

03

Prevent

Fold seasonal care into the plan so the same weather-related issues are not booked from scratch each year.

04

Report

Close the request with a work-order style record of what was done, and photos when they were part of the job.

Consolidation

Stop splitting the property across a pile of trades

One office takes the request for fences and gates, handyman repairs, snow, seasonal lights, and exterior upkeep. Fence, gate, welding, and chain-link work is scheduled through the related Quick Fence Repair practice when that is the work needed. You still deal with one number: 416-900-7998.

  • One intake for the property, not a separate call for each trade
  • A plan you can reuse across a season or a roster of addresses
  • Billing can go to a management office when you put that contact on the request
